**Arcade**



* Stage 2:

Pick a path type of stage, if the player picks the wrong path then their ball will be destroyed. Will send a remoteEvent to the player's client to play a sound.

* Stage 3

A speed ramp similiar to an arcade game. The player has to nudge the ball towards the golf hole, although if it's too fast it falls of the edge and they have to try again.

* Stage 4:

Over the water, meaning that there are multiple tiny bridges which the ball can fall off of and the player has to pass it.

* Stage 5:

Similiar to a real life minigolf course, where you have to get the ball up a mountain and it goes downwards from a spiral.

* Stage 6:

The player goes through a few obstacles (wedges, blocks, etc.), then using a speed boost pad has to get the ball in a net.

* Stage 7:

Spinning and floating triangle obstacles.

* Stage 8:

Pads that dissapear and appear for a specific timeframe.

* Stage 9

Sort of an invisible path. It can be faintly seen, although will flash in specific timeframes.
